Braille Text Field Label Testing Playground

This is a minimal, neutral page for testing how text field labels and editability indicators display on Braille displays. The behavior under test can occur on Braille displays of any cell length (for example 14, 20, 32, 40, or 80 cells). It is most readily apparent on compact displays such as 20-cell models, due to their limited line length. All field labels and values are generic sample text.

Testing steps

  1. Connect a Braille display of any cell length via Bluetooth. Note the number of cells on your display.
  2. Navigate with Rotor or Pan keys to the text fields below.
  3. Observe the editability indicator behavior and how the label displays.
