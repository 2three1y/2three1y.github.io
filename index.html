Experience (for anyone interested)

hey, i’m Chaotic Carter, aka 2three1y. new name, same chaos. the username stays, it has seniority.

also: i’m blind. yes, for real. i make beats i’ve never seen, i write code with VoiceOver reading every line back at me, and i’ve never seen this website. i hear it’s pretty. if it’s ugly, please lie to me.

that’s why everything i build works with a screen reader from the very first commit. not because it’s trendy. because i’m the first user, and if i can’t use it, it isn’t done. it’s a draft with a domain name.

my main quest: poke at tech until it confesses how it works, then turn it into something people can actually use. some days that’s a browser tool. some days it’s chess you play by ear. some days it’s a quiz that roasts your personality. all shipped. all count. no further questions.

side quest that took over: old computer voices, synths and system sounds. startup chimes, classic speech synths, the tiny beeps old hardware makes. if it beeped in 1998, i want it back on speaker. (you might’ve already heard mine.)

and i love tools that live on your own device and don’t need anybody’s permission to work. local-first, decentralized, actually yours. that’s basically Looscid’s whole personality.

Accessibility advocacy (not a paid DLC)

accessibility isn’t a bonus feature you add if there’s time left. it’s the starting line. semantic HTML, focus you can actually see, keyboard for everything, real contrast, structure that makes sense out loud, and no important info hiding behind fiddly extra clicks.

if it only works with a mouse, it’s still in early access. if a div is pretending to be a button, that’s catfishing, and i will find out. VoiceOver snitches. what the hell, div.

Photosensitivity notice

good news first: nothing on this site flashes or moves, and it stays within the three-flash safety limit. this is the one section where i keep a straight face. mostly.

More details (the actually important part)
  • what got checked: every animation, transition and script on this page, plus a frame-by-frame screen recording measured against the WCAG 2.3.1 limits (no more than three flashes in any second, red flashes included).
  • nothing on this site moves or blinks on its own. not even a little. the big color blocks and rings up top are painted on and stay perfectly still.
  • your device’s Reduce Motion setting is respected: if anything that moves ever gets added, it stays still while that setting is on. Calm mode in Settings is on by default too. your settings outrank mine, as they should.
  • sound: the only sound here is my startup chime, and it’s off unless you switch it on (the Startup chime button in the Startup chime section, or in Settings). when it’s on, it’s soft, about two seconds long, and plays once when the page opens (or on your first tap, click or key press if your browser makes sites wait). nothing else on the site makes sound. ironic, considering the playlist.
  • honest limits: this was tested with automated measurement, not a clinical tool like PEAT, and not on every device out there.

if anything here ever flickers, that’s a real bug, not a feature and not a vibe. please report it on GitHub and it jumps to the top of my to-do list, ahead of sleep.
