Looscid

the main quest. forever in beta, on purpose

my local-first, decentralized web workspace, built around one idea: your stuff is yours. your data and your daily tools keep working right on your own device. no account wall, no “please sign in to continue” jumpscare.

it is not trying to replace iOS or Android. it knows its role. its real job is being comfy with a screen reader: predictable landmarks and headings, short chunks, keyboard-first everything, and status updates that get announced instead of hidden. the goal is a workspace that stays chill even when every single thing is being read out loud. quiet server at 3 AM, not ten people unmuting at once.

Startup chime (optional, like sleep)

i made my own startup chime. it stays off unless you turn it on. turn it on and it plays once when the page opens, or on your first tap, click or key press if your browser makes sites wait. it’s soft, about two seconds, made live in your browser, and quiet enough to sit under VoiceOver. your pick is remembered on this device. if your iPhone is on silent, it might stay quiet.

Photosensitivity notice

good news first: nothing on this site flashes or moves, and it stays within the three-flash safety limit. this is the one section where i keep a straight face. mostly.

More details (the actually important part)
  • what got checked: every animation, transition and script on this page, plus a frame-by-frame screen recording measured against the WCAG 2.3.1 limits (no more than three flashes in any second, red flashes included).
  • nothing on this site moves or blinks on its own. not even a little. the big color blocks and rings up top are painted on and stay perfectly still.
  • your device’s Reduce Motion setting is respected: if anything that moves ever gets added, it stays still while that setting is on. Calm mode in Settings is on by default too. your settings outrank mine, as they should.
  • sound: the only sound here is my startup chime, and it’s off unless you switch it on (the Startup chime button in the Startup chime section, or in Settings). when it’s on, it’s soft, about two seconds long, and plays once when the page opens (or on your first tap, click or key press if your browser makes sites wait). nothing else on the site makes sound. ironic, considering the playlist.
  • honest limits: this was tested with automated measurement, not a clinical tool like PEAT, and not on every device out there.

if anything here ever flickers, that’s a real bug, not a feature and not a vibe. please report it on GitHub and it jumps to the top of my to-do list, ahead of sleep.
