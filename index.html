About me

I’m 15. Yes, 15.

I love coding, which means I love tech.

I’m blind.

I usually code by using Poke, Claude, Grok, and whatever else helps.

I also try doing it myself, although it’s quite hard.

I don’t know how to begin this, but here goes.

My love of coding began when I first held my first mobile device, an iPod touch. I loved exploring the apps installed on it. It was nice to tap around, discover what things could do, and feel the possibilities.

That curiosity stuck with me. Even though I can’t see the screen in the same way, the idea of making things that live inside devices has never left.

I’m building lots of things.
